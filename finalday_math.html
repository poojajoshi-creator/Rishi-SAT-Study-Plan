Q8Algebra — Interpreting Linear ModelsHard
A linear model predicts that a company's monthly revenue R (in thousands of £) after t months is given by R = 12.5t + 80. Which statement correctly interprets this model?
  • A The company's revenue was £12,500 when it launched and grows by £80,000 per month.
  • B The company's revenue grows by £12,500 per month with no initial revenue.
  • C The company began with £80,000 in monthly revenue and grows by £12,500 per month.
  • D After 80 months, the company's revenue will be £12,500.
Correct: C
R = 12.5t + 80. At t=0: R=80 (£80,000 initial revenue). Slope=12.5 → grows by £12,500 per month. C correctly interprets both the y-intercept (initial value) and the slope (rate of change). A swaps the values. B = no initial revenue (ignores the 80). D = nonsensical interpretation.

Q11Advanced — DiscriminantHard
How many real solutions does 3x² + 5x + 4 = 0 have?
  • A Two distinct real solutions
  • B One repeated real solution
  • C No real solutions
  • D Cannot be determined without graphing
Correct: C — No real solutions
Discriminant = b²−4ac = 25−4(3)(4) = 25−48 = −23. Since discriminant < 0 → no real solutions (two complex roots). Key: b²−4ac > 0 → two real roots; = 0 → one repeated root; < 0 → no real roots.

  • A 100π cm³
  • B 500π cm³
  • C 600π cm³
  • D 625π cm³
Correct: D — 625π cm³
Volumes of similar 3D figures scale by the CUBE of the linear ratio. Linear ratio = 2:5. Volume ratio = 2³:5³ = 8:125. V(large) = 40π × (125/8) = 40π × 15.625 = 625π cm³.

sin X = opposite/hypotenuse = YZ/XY. YZ = 25 × sin(53°) = 25 × 0.799 ≈ 19.975 ≈ 20.0 cm. (This is a 3-4-5 × 5 triple: 15-20-25 with the 53° angle.)
Q22Trigonometry — Unit CircleHard
Which of the following is equivalent to cos(210°)?
  • A 1/2
  • B √3/2
  • C −1/2
  • D −√3/2
Correct: D — −√3/2
210° = 180° + 30°. In the third quadrant, cosine is negative. cos(210°) = −cos(30°) = −√3/2. Reference angle = 30°. cos(30°) = √3/2 → cos(210°) = −√3/2.

Q26Data — Median & SpreadMedium
The data set {4, 7, 9, 12, 15, 18, 22} has a median of 12. If the value 22 is removed, which of the following statements is true?
  • A The median increases and the range decreases.
  • B The median stays the same and the range stays the same.
  • C The median decreases and the range decreases.
  • D The median stays the same and the range decreases.
Correct: C
Without 22: {4,7,9,12,15,18}. New median = average of 9 and 12 = 10.5 (decreased from 12). Range: was 22−4=18, now 18−4=14 (decreased). Both median and range decrease → C.
Q27Data — ProbabilityHard
A spinner has sections coloured red, blue, and green. The probability of landing on red is 0.35 and blue is 0.45. In 200 spins, what is the expected number of times it lands on green?
  • A 30

  • D 45
Correct: C — 40
P(green) = 1−0.35−0.45 = 0.20. Expected green spins = 200 × 0.20 = 40.
