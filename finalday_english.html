Q1TransitionsMedium
Studies in the 1980s established that exercise significantly improves cardiovascular health by strengthening the heart muscle and improving circulation. Recent research has revealed that regular physical activity also triggers the release of neurotrophic factors that promote the growth of new neurons in the hippocampus, a region critical for learning and memory.
Which transition best connects the two sentences?
  • A However, recent research
  • B Therefore, recent research
  • C More recently, research has revealed
  • D For example, recent research
Correct: C — Addition
S1: exercise helps the heart. S2: exercise also helps the brain. Both are benefits of exercise — same direction, adding a new category of benefit. "More recently" signals addition with a temporal shift. A (However) = contrast — wrong, both are benefits. B (Therefore) = result — S2 isn't a consequence of S1. D (For example) = S2 isn't an example of cardiovascular benefit.
