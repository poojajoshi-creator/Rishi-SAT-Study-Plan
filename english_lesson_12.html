Concept 12 / 27Expression of Ideas
Transitions
Example
Sentence B illustrates the same point Sentence A makes — but with a specific, concrete case. The second sentence doesn't add a new argument; it shows what the first sentence looks like in reality.
~1 per moduleTarget: 25 secTest: is S2 a specific case of S1?
🔍
Example transitions
Step 1
The words

for example · for instance · to illustrate · as an illustration · specifically · a case in point is · consider · take, for instance

The structural test: Sentence A makes a general claim. Sentence B gives a specific case that falls under that claim. You can say "Sentence B is an instance of what Sentence A describes." If yes → example transition.

Why it matters: Addition ("furthermore") introduces a NEW separate point. Example ("for instance") illustrates the SAME point. If you pick "furthermore" when the correct answer is "for instance," you're claiming B is a new argument when it's actually evidence for A.

Example vs Addition — the side-by-side

Example: "Many foods considered traditionally Italian actually have foreign origins. For instance, the tomato was introduced to Italy from the Americas in the 16th century."
→ The tomato IS a case of a food with foreign origins. S2 illustrates S1.

Addition: "Italian cuisine has absorbed many foreign ingredients. Furthermore, Italian cooking techniques have also evolved through contact with French culinary traditions."
→ Two separate points about Italian cuisine — ingredients AND techniques. S2 adds a new argument, not an example of S1.

Quick test: Can you replace the blank with "and this is seen in the case of..."? → example. Can you replace it with "and also..."? → addition.

⭐
Strategy
Step 2
1
Identify the general claim in S1. State it in your own words: "S1 claims that [X in general]."
2
Ask: is S2 a specific instance of that general claim? "S2 describes [specific case]. Is [specific case] an example of [X in general]?" If yes → example word.
3
If S2 introduces a completely different point that also supports the same conclusion (not an illustration of S1 itself) → addition word instead.
⚠️
Traps
Step 3
Trap 1
"Furthermore" for an illustration
S2 is clearly a specific case of S1, but students pick "furthermore" because they think B is adding a new point. Check: is B an instance of A's claim, or a separate claim? Instance = "for example."
Trap 2
"For example" for a cause-effect
S2 follows from S1 causally — but students read it as an illustration. If A caused B, use a result word. "For example" only works when B is a specific case that falls under A's general category.
Trap 3
Confusing "specifically" with "in contrast"
"Specifically" (= more precisely, to narrow to a particular case) can look like "in contrast" when a specific subgroup differs from the general trend. If S2 narrows S1 (same direction), it's "specifically." If S2 opposes S1, it's "however."

Worked examples
Step 4
Example 1 — Clear illustrationEasy
Many animals have developed remarkable physiological adaptations to survive in extreme environments. _______, the tardigrade — a microscopic animal found in every ecosystem on Earth — can survive temperatures ranging from −273°C to 150°C, intense radiation, and even the vacuum of space by entering a state of suspended animation called cryptobiosis.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. Furthermore
  • B. However
  • C. For example
  • D. As a result
C. S1: animals have extreme-environment adaptations (general). S2: the tardigrade IS one such animal (specific case). Tardigrade = an example of S1's claim. A (Furthermore) would make the tardigrade a new separate argument — but it's a concrete illustration of the existing claim.
Example 2 — Example vs AdditionMedium
Governments have increasingly used financial incentives to encourage behaviour change among citizens. _______, several countries now offer tax rebates to households that install solar panels or purchase electric vehicles, redirecting consumer choices toward lower-carbon options without mandating them.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. Moreover
  • B. For instance
  • C. Nevertheless
  • D. Therefore
B. S1: governments use financial incentives to change behaviour (general). S2: solar and EV tax rebates are exactly that — a specific case of financial incentives changing behaviour. "For instance" names this relationship. A (Moreover) = new separate point of greater weight — wrong, S2 illustrates rather than adds.
Example 3 — "Specifically" for narrowingHard
The data showed that employee satisfaction declined across the organisation following the restructuring. _______, workers in the customer service division — who faced the largest team consolidations and role changes — reported the sharpest drop in satisfaction scores, with 68% rating their experience as "significantly worse."
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. However
  • B. Furthermore
  • C. More specifically
  • D. Nevertheless
C. S1: satisfaction dropped across the organisation. S2: customer service had the worst drop. S2 narrows the general finding to a specific subset — "more specifically" signals this zoom-in. A (However) = contrast — but S2 doesn't oppose S1, it confirms it with a specific subgroup. B (Furthermore) = a new separate point — wrong, S2 drills into the same finding.

Practice — 5 questions
Step 5
Practice 1
Colonial powers often imposed borders that cut across existing ethnic, linguistic, and cultural boundaries with little regard for the communities affected. _______, the Sykes-Picot Agreement of 1916 divided the Arab world between British and French spheres of influence, fragmenting Kurdish, Arab, and Assyrian communities across the newly drawn lines.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. Nevertheless
  • B. For instance
  • C. As a result
  • D. Furthermore
B. S1: colonial borders ignored existing communities (general). Sykes-Picot = a specific instance of exactly this. "For instance."
Practice 2
Misinformation spreads faster on social media than factual corrections. _______, a 2018 MIT study of Twitter found that false news travelled six times faster and reached significantly more users than accurate news stories covering the same events.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. However
  • B. Consequently
  • C. Indeed
  • D. Moreover
C. "Indeed" signals that S2 confirms or intensifies S1 with specific evidence. The MIT study proves the general claim. This is a variant of the example relationship — not a new point, but concrete evidence for the existing claim. "In fact" or "indeed" works here where "for example" would also fit.
Practice 3 — Example or Addition?
Invasive plant species can fundamentally alter soil chemistry in ways that prevent native plants from re-establishing. _______, the spread of Japanese knotweed releases chemicals into the soil that suppress the growth of competing species, a process called allelopathy.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. Furthermore
  • B. However
  • C. For example
  • D. Nevertheless
C. S1: invasives alter soil chemistry, stopping native regrowth (general mechanism). Japanese knotweed allelopathy = a specific case of exactly that mechanism. "For example." A (Furthermore) would make knotweed a new independent claim — but it's an illustration of S1's mechanism.
Practice 4
The history of computing is filled with technologies that were initially dismissed as impractical or unnecessary by industry experts. _______, IBM's president Thomas Watson is widely quoted as having predicted in 1943 that there was a world market for perhaps five computers.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. Therefore
  • B. Nevertheless
  • C. To illustrate
  • D. As a result
C. S1: computing history has examples of dismissed technologies. Watson's quote = one such example. "To illustrate" names this relationship explicitly.
Practice 5 — Hard
Architectural decisions can have profound effects on social behaviour within buildings. _______, research on hospital design has found that placing nursing stations at central locations with sightlines to patient rooms significantly reduces response times to patient emergencies while also decreasing nurses' walking distances by up to 30%.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. Moreover
  • B. However
  • C. Consequently
  • D. For instance
D. S1: architecture affects social behaviour (general). S2: nursing station placement affects nurse behaviour and patient outcomes (specific case). The hospital design research is an illustration of S1's general claim. A (Moreover) = new point of greater weight — wrong. C (Consequently) = causal — architecture didn't cause the research; the research illustrates the claim about architecture.

Transitions — Example · Everything you need
The words
for example · for instance · to illustrate · specifically · more specifically · consider · indeed · in fact
The test
Is S2 a specific case of what S1 claims? "S2 is an instance of S1." → example. S2 is a separate new argument → addition.
Example vs Addition
"For example" = S2 illustrates S1. "Furthermore" = S2 is a completely new separate supporting point. Both same direction — the question is illustration vs new argument.
"Specifically" for narrowing
"More specifically" / "specifically" zoom into a particular subset of S1's general claim. Not a new point — a narrower view of the same point.
Trap
"For example" when B causally follows A. If A produces B → result word. "For example" only works when B is a case that falls under A's category.
Signal in S2
S2 names a specific case, study, person, place, or event that the general claim in S1 would predict or include → example.
