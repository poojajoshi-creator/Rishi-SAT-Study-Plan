Transitions
Addition
Sentence B continues in the same direction as Sentence A — adding a new point, building on the same idea, or extending the argument further.
~1 per moduleTarget: 25 secTest: can you put "and also" between them?
➕
Addition transitions
Step 1
The words

furthermore · additionally · moreover · in addition · also · as well · besides · on top of that · what is more

The test: Replace the blank with "and also." If both sentences point in the same direction and B adds a new supporting point, it's addition.

Degree matters: "Moreover" and "furthermore" often introduce a point that is MORE significant than A — not just another point, but a stronger one. "Additionally" and "in addition" are neutral additions. "Besides" often adds a secondary or minor point.

Addition vs Example — the key distinction

Addition introduces a NEW, SEPARATE point that supports the same claim.
Example: "Exercise improves mood. Furthermore, it reduces the risk of heart disease." Two different benefits.

Example (Lesson 12) illustrates the SAME point with a specific case.
Example: "Exercise has many benefits. For instance, it has been shown to improve mood." The same point, now made concrete.

Test: is the second sentence a new independent point (addition) or an illustration of the first (example)?

⭐
Strategy
Step 2
1
Check direction: do both sentences support the same claim or point the same way? If yes — addition or example. If opposite — contrast. If B follows from A — result.
2
Check if B is a new point or an illustration. New independent point → addition word. Specific case of A → example word.
3
Check degree: Is B at least as important or more important than A? → "moreover/furthermore." Is B a minor or supplementary point? → "additionally/besides."
⚠️
Traps
Step 3
Trap 1
Addition vs Example
"Furthermore" for an illustration. B gives a specific case of A (example), not a new point (addition). "For example" would be right. The question: is B a new argument or a concrete case of A?
Trap 2
Addition vs Result
"Furthermore" when B follows from A. If A causes B, use a result word. If A and B are just two independent supporting points, use addition.
Trap 3
Same direction ≠ always addition
Two sentences can point in the same direction without one ADDING to the other. Restatement (in other words), conclusion (therefore), or elaboration can look like addition but need different words.

Worked examples
Step 4
Example 1Easy
Renewable energy sources such as solar and wind produce no direct carbon emissions during operation. _______, they are becoming increasingly cost-competitive with fossil fuels as technology improves and production scales up.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. However
  • B. For example
  • C. Furthermore
  • D. As a result
C. S1: renewables = clean. S2: renewables = increasingly affordable. Two different, independent advantages — addition. B (For example) would make the cost point an illustration of zero emissions — wrong, it's a separate benefit.
Example 2 — Addition vs ExampleMedium
Many invasive species alter ecosystems by outcompeting native plants and animals for resources. _______, the zebra mussel, introduced to the Great Lakes in the 1980s, has dramatically reduced populations of native mussels by filtering phytoplankton from the water at extraordinary rates.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. Furthermore
  • B. However
  • C. For instance
  • D. Therefore
C. S1: invasive species outcompete native species (general claim). S2: zebra mussel outcompetes native mussels (specific case). S2 illustrates S1 with a concrete example. "For instance" = illustration. A (Furthermore) would imply the zebra mussel is a new, separate point — but it's an example of the exact claim made in S1.
Example 3 — "Moreover" for weightHard
The proposed development would destroy the last remaining habitat for three species of native birds in the region. _______, it would eliminate a wetland area that currently provides natural flood protection for the downstream community of 40,000 residents.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. In contrast
  • B. For instance
  • C. Additionally
  • D. Moreover
D. S1: harm to bird habitat. S2: harm to human flood protection for 40,000 people. Both are harms (same direction — addition), but the human safety risk is arguably MORE significant than the bird habitat. "Moreover" signals a point of equal or greater weight. C (Additionally) is neutral addition — acceptable but "moreover" better captures the escalating seriousness.

Practice — 5 questions
Step 5
Practice 1
The new legislation would reduce corporate tax rates. _______, it would simplify the filing process by eliminating dozens of deductions that currently require extensive documentation.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. However
  • B. In addition
  • C. For example
  • D. Consequently
B. Two separate benefits of the legislation: lower taxes + simpler filing. Independent new point = addition.
Practice 2
The study found that students who ate breakfast performed better on morning assessments than those who did not. _______, students who ate a high-protein breakfast showed the largest improvements in concentration and working memory.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. Nevertheless
  • B. For example
  • C. More specifically
  • D. However
C. S1: breakfast eaters do better. S2: high-protein breakfast eaters do best. S2 narrows and specifies S1 — it's a more specific subset of the same finding. "More specifically" = elaboration/specification, a form of addition that drills into detail.
Practice 3
Sleep deprivation impairs memory consolidation, making it harder to retain information learned during the day. _______, it reduces reaction time and increases the risk of errors in tasks requiring sustained attention.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. As a result
  • B. In contrast
  • C. Furthermore
  • D. For instance
C. Two separate cognitive harms of sleep deprivation: memory + reaction time. New independent point = addition. A (As a result) would make the reaction time impairment a consequence of the memory impairment — not the relationship here.
Practice 4
Many plant-based foods are rich in dietary fibre, which supports healthy digestion. _______, legumes such as lentils and chickpeas are excellent sources of fibre, providing 15–16 grams per cooked cup.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. Furthermore
  • B. However
  • C. For example
  • D. As a result
C. S1: plant-based foods are fibre-rich (general). S2: lentils and chickpeas are fibre-rich (specific case). Specific cases of a general statement = example, not addition.
Practice 5 — Hard
The Arctic is warming at four times the global average rate, causing permafrost to thaw and release stored carbon into the atmosphere. _______, this accelerated warming is disrupting the jet stream in ways that produce extreme weather events at lower latitudes — including the flooding and heat waves increasingly experienced in Europe and North America.
Which transition best completes the text?
  • A. For instance
  • B. As a result
  • C. However
  • D. Moreover
D. S1: Arctic warming releases carbon (one major consequence). S2: Arctic warming disrupts jet stream → extreme weather elsewhere (second, arguably more far-reaching consequence). Two separate significant consequences, with S2 having broader global impact = "moreover." B (As a result) would make the jet stream disruption a consequence of the carbon release — but both are separate consequences of the warming, not one causing the other.

The words

The test
Replace with "and also." Same direction + new separate point → addition. Same direction + specific case → example (for instance).
Degree
"Moreover / furthermore" = point of equal or greater significance. "Additionally" = neutral new point. "Besides" = minor/secondary point.
