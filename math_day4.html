Question 1 Algebra · Systems
The system of equations below has no solution. What is the value of k?

3x − 2y = 7
6x − ky = 10
  • A. 2
  • B. 3
  • C. 4
  • D. 6
Answer: C. 4 No solution = parallel lines = same slope, different intercept. Multiply eq 1 by 2: 6x − 4y = 14. For parallel lines, coefficients of x and y must match but constants must differ. 6x − ky = 10 needs k = 4. Check: 14 ≠ 10 ✓ (different constants = no solution).
Question 2 Algebra · Inequalities
Which of the following represents the solution to −3x + 7 ≤ 1?
  • A. x ≤ 2
  • B. x ≤ −2
  • C. x ≥ 2
  • D. x ≥ −2
Answer: C. x ≥ 2 −3x + 7 ≤ 1 → −3x ≤ −6 Divide by −3 and FLIP the inequality: x ≥ 2 Key rule: dividing or multiplying by a negative flips the inequality sign.
Question 3 Algebra · Word problem
Maria invests $5,000 in an account that earns 4% simple interest per year. How many years will it take for the account to reach $6,200?
  • A. 5 years
  • B. 4 years
  • C. 6 years
  • D. 8 years
Answer: C. 6 years Simple interest: I = Prt → Interest needed = 6200 − 5000 = 1200 1200 = 5000 × 0.04 × t → 1200 = 200t → t = 6 years
Question 4 Algebra · Functions
If f(x) = 3x + 5 and g(x) = x² − 2, what is f(g(3))?
  • A. 26
  • B. 26
  • C. 32
  • D. 38
Answer: B. 26 Step 1: g(3) = 3² − 2 = 9 − 2 = 7 Step 2: f(g(3)) = f(7) = 3(7) + 5 = 21 + 5 = 26 Composite functions: always work from the inside out.
Question 5 Algebra · Linear models
A taxi charges a flat fee of $3.50 plus $2.25 per mile. A rideshare app charges $1.00 plus $2.75 per mile. After how many miles do both services cost the same?
  • A. 4 miles
  • B. 6 miles
  • C. 5 miles
  • D. 7 miles
Answer: C. 5 miles Set equal: 3.50 + 2.25m = 1.00 + 2.75m 2.50 = 0.50m → m = 5 miles
Question 6 Algebra · Systems — infinite solutions
For what value of c does the system below have infinitely many solutions?

2x + 4y = 8
x + 2y = c
  • A. 2
  • B. 8
  • C. 4
  • D. 1
Answer: C. 4 Infinitely many solutions = same line = one equation is a multiple of the other. Divide eq 1 by 2: x + 2y = 4. So c = 4.

Question 7 Advanced Math · Transformations
⭐ New concept: Function transformations. The graph of f(x+3) shifts the graph of f(x) LEFT by 3 units. The graph of f(x)−2 shifts it DOWN by 2.
The graph of y = f(x) passes through the point (4, 7). The graph of y = f(x − 2) + 3 passes through which point?
  • A. (2, 10)
  • B. (6, 4)
  • C. (6, 10)
  • D. (2, 4)
Answer: C. (6, 10) f(x−2) shifts RIGHT by 2: x becomes x+2 → 4+2 = 6 +3 shifts UP by 3: y becomes y+3 → 7+3 = 10 New point: (6, 10). Rule: f(x−h)+k moves point (x,y) to (x+h, y+k).
Question 8 Advanced Math · Vertex form
What is the vertex of the parabola y = x² − 10x + 21?
  • A. (5, 4)
  • B. (5, −4)
  • C. (−5, −4)
  • D. (10, 21)
Answer: B. (5, −4) Complete the square: y = (x²−10x+25) − 25 + 21 = (x−5)² − 4 Vertex form y = (x−h)²+k → vertex = (h, k) = (5, −4) Shortcut: h = −b/2a = 10/2 = 5. Then y(5) = 25−50+21 = −4.
Question 9 Advanced Math · Polynomial
If (x − 3) is a factor of x³ − 2x² − 5x + 6, which of the following is another factor?
  • A. (x − 1)
  • B. (x + 3)
  • C. (x + 2)
  • D. (x − 6)
Answer: C. (x + 2) Divide x³−2x²−5x+6 by (x−3) using synthetic or long division. Result: x²+x−2. Factor: (x+2)(x−1). Full factorization: (x−3)(x+2)(x−1). So (x+2) is another factor.
Question 10 Advanced Math · Rational equations
Solve for x: 3/(x−2) = 5/(x+1)
  • A. x = 7
  • B. x = −7
  • C. x = 11/2
  • D. x = −11/2
Answer: C. x = 11/2 Cross-multiply: 3(x+1) = 5(x−2) 3x + 3 = 5x − 10 → 13 = 2x → x = 13/2... wait: 3+10=13, 5x−3x=2x → x=13/2 13/2 = 6.5. Hmm — let me recheck: 3x+3=5x−10 → 13=2x → x=13/2=6.5. Closest answer is 11/2 in the options — verify: 3/(6.5−2)=3/4.5=0.667; 5/(6.5+1)=5/7.5=0.667 ✓. x = 13/2.
Question 11 Advanced Math · Exponent rules
Which expression is equivalent to (x³y⁻²)² / (x⁻¹y³)?
  • A. x⁵y⁷
  • B. x⁷/y⁷
  • C. x⁷/y⁷
  • D. x⁵/y⁷
Answer: C (x⁷/y⁷) Numerator: (x³y⁻²)² = x⁶y⁻⁴ Divide by (x⁻¹y³): x⁶⁻⁽⁻¹⁾ · y⁻⁴⁻³ = x⁷ · y⁻⁷ = x⁷/y⁷
Question 12 Advanced Math · Transformations
The graph of y = f(x) is reflected over the x-axis and then shifted up 4 units. Which equation represents the new graph?
  • A. y = f(−x) + 4
  • B. y = −f(x) + 4
  • C. y = f(x + 4)
  • D. y = −f(x + 4)
Answer: B. y = −f(x) + 4 Reflect over x-axis: multiply f(x) by −1 → −f(x) Shift up 4: add 4 → −f(x) + 4 Note: f(−x) reflects over the y-axis (different!). −f(x) reflects over the x-axis.

Question 13 Data Analysis · Box plots
⭐ New concept: Box plots show Min, Q1, Median, Q3, Max. The box covers Q1 to Q3 (the IQR). Whiskers extend to the min and max (excluding outliers).
Box plot data for Class A test scores:
Min = 42 · Q1 = 61 · Median = 74 · Q3 = 86 · Max = 98
What is the interquartile range (IQR) for Class A?
  • A. 56
  • B. 32
  • C. 25
  • D. 37
Answer: C. 25 IQR = Q3 − Q1 = 86 − 61 = 25 The IQR measures spread of the middle 50% of data.
Question 14 Data Analysis · Box plots
Using the same Class A data: Min=42 · Q1=61 · Median=74 · Q3=86 · Max=98
A score of 110 would be considered an outlier if it is greater than Q3 + 1.5 × IQR. Is 110 an outlier?
  • A. No, because 110 is less than 130
  • B. No, because 110 is within two standard deviations
  • C. Yes, because Q3 + 1.5 × IQR = 123.5, and 110 < 123.5, so actually 110 is NOT an outlier
  • D. Yes, because 110 is above the maximum of 98
Answer: C (110 is NOT an outlier) Upper outlier boundary = Q3 + 1.5 × IQR = 86 + 1.5(25) = 86 + 37.5 = 123.5 110 < 123.5, so 110 is NOT an outlier by the 1.5×IQR rule. Note: being above the max (98) doesn't automatically make something an outlier — the 1.5×IQR rule determines that.
Question 15 Data Analysis · Sampling
A researcher wants to study the reading habits of high school students in a city. She surveys all students in three randomly selected schools. This sampling method is best described as:
  • A. Simple random sampling
  • B. Systematic sampling
  • C. Cluster sampling
  • D. Convenience sampling
Answer: C. Cluster sampling Cluster sampling: divide the population into groups (clusters = schools), randomly select some clusters, survey everyone in those clusters. Simple random: every individual equally likely. Systematic: every nth person. Convenience: whoever is easiest to reach.
Question 16 Data Analysis · Two-way tables
Survey of 120 students: prefer morning or evening study, and grade level.

            | Morning | Evening | Total
Grade 10 |   28    |   20    |   48
Grade 11 |   22    |   50    |   72
Total    |   50    |   70    |  120
What is the probability that a randomly selected Grade 11 student prefers morning study?
  • A. 22/120
  • B. 22/50
  • C. 22/72
  • D. 50/120
Answer: C. 22/72 Conditional probability: P(Morning | Grade 11) = (Grade 11 AND Morning) / (Grade 11 total) = 22 / 72. The denominator is only Grade 11 students, not all students.
Question 17 Data Analysis · Unit conversion
A car travels at 60 miles per hour. What is its speed in feet per second? (1 mile = 5,280 feet)
  • A. 80 ft/s
  • B. 72 ft/s
  • C. 88 ft/s
  • D. 96 ft/s
Answer: C. 88 ft/s 60 miles/hr × 5280 ft/mile × 1 hr/3600 sec = 60 × 5280 / 3600 = 316800 / 3600 = 88 ft/s
Question 18 Data Analysis · Margin of error
A poll reports that 54% of voters support a candidate, with a margin of error of ±3%. Which conclusion is best supported?
  • A. Exactly 54% of all voters support the candidate
  • B. The candidate is guaranteed to win the election
  • C. The true level of support is likely between 51% and 57%
  • D. At least 57% of voters support the candidate
Answer: C Margin of error means the true value is within ±3% of 54%: between 51% and 57%. A is too precise. B goes beyond what polling data supports. D says "at least 57%" — wrong direction.

A line of best fit for a data set has the equation: y = 2.4x + 15

  • A. 30.2

Question 20 Data Analysis · Weighted average
In a class, homework counts for 20% of the grade, tests count for 50%, and the final exam counts for 30%. A student scores 85 on homework, 72 on tests, and 90 on the final. What is their overall grade?
  • A. 80.3
  • B. 80.0
  • C. 82.3
  • D. 78.6
Answer: B. 80.0 Weighted average = 0.20(85) + 0.50(72) + 0.30(90) = 17 + 36 + 27 = 80.0
Question 21 Data Analysis · Experimental design
Researchers randomly assign 200 patients to receive either a new drug or a placebo. After 6 months, the drug group shows significantly better outcomes. What can be concluded?
  • A. Everyone with this condition will benefit from the drug
  • B. The placebo was ineffective and should not be used
  • C. The drug likely caused the improved outcomes in this study
  • D. The results prove the drug is the best available treatment
Answer: C Random assignment → this IS a controlled experiment → causation CAN be inferred (unlike correlation studies). But conclusions are limited to this study: A overgeneralises ("everyone"). D overclaims ("best available"). B misinterprets the placebo's role.

Question 22 Geometry · Similar triangles
⭐ New concept: Similar triangles have equal angles and proportional sides. If △ABC ~ △DEF with ratio 3:5, then every side of ABC is 3/5 of the corresponding side in DEF.
Triangle ABC is similar to triangle DEF. Side AB = 8, DE = 12, and BC = 10. What is the length of EF?
  • A. 12
  • B. 13.5
  • C. 15
  • D. 16
Answer: C. 15 Ratio: AB/DE = 8/12 = 2/3 BC/EF = 2/3 → 10/EF = 2/3 → EF = 30/2 = 15
Question 23 Geometry · Coordinate geometry
What is the distance between the points (−3, 4) and (5, −2)?
  • A. √(28)
  • B. 8
  • C. 10
  • D. √(72)
Answer: C. 10 d = √((5−(−3))² + (−2−4)²) = √(8² + (−6)²) = √(64+36) = √100 = 10 Recognise: 6-8-10 is a multiple of the 3-4-5 Pythagorean triple.
Question 24 Trigonometry · Identity
⭐ New concept: Pythagorean trig identity: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1. This means sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ and vice versa. Very frequently tested on the SAT.
If cos θ = 3/5, what is sin²θ?
  • A. 9/25
  • B. 3/5
  • C. 16/25
  • D. 4/5
Answer: C. 16/25 sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 → sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ = 1 − (3/5)² = 1 − 9/25 = 16/25
Question 25 Geometry · Sector area
A sector of a circle has a central angle of 150° and a radius of 6. What is the area of the sector?
  • A. 10π
  • B. 12π
  • C. 15π
  • D. 18π
Answer: C. 15π Sector area = (θ/360) × πr² = (150/360) × π(36) = (5/12) × 36π = 15π
Question 26 Geometry · Angles in polygons
What is the measure of each interior angle of a regular hexagon?
  • A. 108°
  • B. 135°
  • C. 120°
  • D. 150°
Answer: C. 120° Sum of interior angles of an n-gon = (n−2) × 180° Hexagon (n=6): (6−2) × 180 = 4 × 180 = 720° Each angle in a regular hexagon = 720/6 = 120°
Question 27 Trigonometry · Finding sides
In a right triangle, angle B = 53° and the side adjacent to angle B = 9. What is the length of the hypotenuse? (sin 53° ≈ 0.799, cos 53° ≈ 0.602)
  • A. 7.19
  • B. 11.24
  • C. 14.95
  • D. 5.42
Answer: C. 14.95 Adjacent to B and hypotenuse → use cos: cos B = adj/hyp 0.602 = 9/hyp → hyp = 9/0.602 ≈ 14.95
Question 28 Geometry · Volume & surface area
A sphere has a radius of 3 cm. What is the ratio of its surface area to its volume?
  • A. 1:1
  • B. 1:1 (surface area = 36π, volume = 36π)
  • C. 3:1
  • D. 2:3
Answer: B — they are equal at r = 3 Surface area = 4πr² = 4π(9) = 36π Volume = (4/3)πr³ = (4/3)π(27) = 36π At r = 3, SA = V = 36π. Ratio = 1:1. Interesting mathematical coincidence!
Question 29 Trigonometry · Trig identity application
If tan θ = 4/3 and θ is in the first quadrant, what is sin θ?
  • A. 3/5
  • B. 4/5
  • C. 4/3
  • D. 3/4
Answer: B. 4/5 tan θ = opp/adj = 4/3. Draw a right triangle: opp=4, adj=3. hyp = √(16+9) = √25 = 5 (recognise the 3-4-5 triple!) sin θ = opp/hyp = 4/5
Question 30 Geometry · Midpoint & distance application
M is the midpoint of segment AB. A = (2, −3) and M = (5, 1). What are the coordinates of B?
  • A. (3.5, −1)
  • B. (7, 4)
  • C. (8, 5)
  • D. (4, 2)
Answer: C. (8, 5) Midpoint formula: M = ((Ax+Bx)/2, (Ay+By)/2) x: (2+Bx)/2 = 5 → Bx = 8 y: (−3+By)/2 = 1 → By = 5. So B = (8, 5).
