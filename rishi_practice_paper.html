Q1Expression of Ideas — TransitionsMedium
For centuries, coral reefs were considered stable ecosystems capable of recovering from periodic disturbances such as storms. Researchers now understand that rising ocean temperatures cause bleaching events that can kill entire reef systems within weeks.
Which transition best connects the two sentences?
  • A Furthermore, researchers now understand
  • B For instance, researchers now understand
  • C However, researchers now understand
  • D Consequently, researchers now understand
Correct: C — Contrast
S1: reefs were stable and resilient. S2: reefs are now understood to be highly vulnerable. Direct contradiction of prior belief → contrast. "However" signals the pivot. A (Furthermore) = addition — same direction. B = example. D = result — the vulnerability didn't result from the stability belief.
Q2Expression of Ideas — TransitionsMedium
The city's new cycling infrastructure reduced traffic congestion along the main arterial roads. Commute times for drivers fell by an average of eleven minutes per journey.
Which transition best connects the two sentences?
  • A Nevertheless, commute times
  • B In contrast, commute times
  • C As a result, commute times
  • D For example, commute times
Correct: C — Cause/Result
Reduced congestion → shorter commutes. S2 is the direct consequence of S1. "As a result." A = concession. B = contrast — both point same way. D = example — commute times are a consequence, not an illustration.

Q27Standard English Conventions — Subject-Verb AgreementHard
Neither the senior partners nor the managing director were informed of the restructuring plan before it was announced to the entire firm.
Which choice is grammatically correct?
  • A No change — "were" is correct
  • B was
  • C have been
  • D are
Correct: B — "was"

Q31Geometry — Arc Length & SectorsMedium
A circle has a radius of 9 cm. A sector has a central angle of 140°. What is the arc length of this sector, in terms of π?
  • A 5π cm
  • B 7π cm
  • C 9π cm
  • D 14π cm
Correct: B — 7π cm
Arc length = (θ/360°) × 2πr = (140/360) × 2π(9) = (7/18) × 18π = 7π cm.
Q32Geometry — Similar TrianglesMedium
Triangle ABC is similar to Triangle DEF. The sides of Triangle ABC measure 6, 8, and 10. The shortest side of Triangle DEF measures 15. What is the perimeter of Triangle DEF?
  • A 48
  • B 54
  • C 60
  • D 75
